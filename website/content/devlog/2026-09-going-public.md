Kreative Kompas Engine went public on GitHub under the MIT licence on
3 September, and September turned it from a set of demos into something you
can make a game with.

## What landed

- **Lua scripting** with Garry's Mod-style hooks and timers, hot reload and
  a sandbox, and a first game written only in Lua: break the targets.
- **Movement** on PointDown's principles: vaulting, climbing, ledge hang and
  shimmy, corners and jumping off walls, and characters from asset packs
  retargeted onto the engine's animations.
- **Destruction** that meets the rest of the world: FEMFX shards knock Jolt
  crates about, and ragdolls join in.
- **Networking**: host, join, LAN discovery, server-authoritative physics.
- **Audio** with synthesized impacts, occlusion and a sound visualizer for
  deaf and hard-of-hearing players.
- **The sandbox** saves levels and shows thumbnails of your asset packs.
- **A starter template and tutorials**, this website and the
  [documentation](/docs/).
