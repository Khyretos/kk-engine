vitruvian_voluptuous.glb
========================
A full-figured woman for the jiggle demo (games/jiggle_demo): the Vitruvian body
from CharMorph (https://github.com/Upliner/CharMorph-Vitruvian), shaped with its
own morphs (female, endomorph, breast size, glutes, hips, thighs; the breast,
glute and hip morphs pushed to 1.7x their slider range), its scanned skin colour
(light, 1024 px tiles in one 2048 atlas), eyes and mouth textures, and the
bandeau and shorts that mark its "censor" material.

Source licence: CC0 (public domain), "license: CC0" in the repository's
config.yaml.
Authors: Sean Buckley, Olaf Delgado-Friedrichs (+ SirMaxim, Upliner, AgentA1CR,
Benjamin McDonnell, Mindfront). Based on a modified Antonia Polygon, relicensed
with its author's permission (the repository's README).
The .glb itself: built by tools/vitruvian/build_body.py. It carries Vitruvian's own
Mixamo skeleton (52 bones, placed on this body's shape) and skin weights
(weights/mixamo.npz), the bandeau and shorts as cloth shells cut from its skin.
Credit is welcome, not required.
